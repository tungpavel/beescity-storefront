BeesCity Reels Scheduler

A private internal tool used by BeesCity to schedule Instagram Reels for the beescity.memories account via the Meta Content Publishing API.

This app is not available to the public. It is used exclusively by the BeesCity account owner to automate content publishing.

Permissions used

  • instagram_basic — to identify the connected Instagram Business Account
  • instagram_content_publish — to schedule and publish Reels to the BeesCity Instagram account
  • pages_show_list — to retrieve the Facebook Page linked to the Instagram account
  • pages_read_engagement — to read Page data and identify the linked Instagram Business Account

Data deletion

This app does not store user data on any external server. All data (access tokens, schedule) is stored locally on the owner's device.

To revoke access, go to Facebook Settings → Security and Login → Apps and Websites, find BeesCity Reels Scheduler and click Remove.
